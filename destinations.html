Travel Tips

Prepare for a comfortable and enjoyable adventure.

What should I pack?

Pack comfortable clothes, walking shoes, sunscreen, a hat, insect repellent, personal medication, and a camera.

When is the best time to visit?

Wildlife viewing is possible throughout the year. The best time depends on your destination and interests.

What should I wear on safari?

Choose comfortable, neutral-coloured clothing, a hat, and closed shoes for outdoor activities.

How can I stay safe around wildlife?

Follow your guide's instructions, remain inside the safari vehicle unless told otherwise, and never approach or feed wild animals.
